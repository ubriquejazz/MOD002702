## Club de baloncesto
Nuestro club necesita tener almacenada la información de todos los socios. Sobre cada socio se necesita saber su nombre completo, su estatura, su edad y su localidad de residencia. Cada socio tendrá, además, un número de identificación único que será el número de socio.

Nuestra aplicación consistirá en varias páginas JSP que van a permitir ver un listado con los datos de todos los socios, dar de alta un nuevo miembro en el club, y también borrar o modificar los datos de un determinado socio.

## The as and when foundation
To build a web application that provides users the possibility to come together. There are two categories of users - inquirers and contributors. The inquirers are people who ask for help and assistance, this may be financial, practical or professional, as and when required. The contributors are people who offer help and contribute to their community, then highlighting and acknowledging their skills and how they have made a difference.

**Contributors**
* Companies offering help or allowing staff time off to contribute to their communities – great publicity. Businesses everywhere have the resources to provide a major positive impact in their communities. Whether it’s by supporting a local charity, the staff voluntarily teaching skills to others or sponsoring an event, participating in acts of “giving back” has extensive benefits for a business well beyond the obvious benefit of helping the community.

* Anyone willing to give their resources, money, help and guidance for the good of others.
* Any professional who would like to practice their new skills on non-paying.
* Charities offering their services of help to those that fit into their remit. Charities helping and collaborating with other charities.
* School Leavers and unemployed who would like to contribute and add community experience and testimonials to their CV.

The web application will allow anyone to fill in a profile with a photo (it may be uploaded from LinkedIn) to explain who they are, the skills, experience or knowledge they would like to offer i.e an accountant offering financial advice or a coach offering personal development skills, and their availability. Each exchange of service is acknowledged and added to their profile, this can then be linked to all social media sites, spreading the word of their contribution and the inquirers appreciation.

**Inquirer**
Many of life's obstacles can't be tackled alone - we simply don't live long enough to learn how to do everything on our own. Whether you're looking for a little assistance or seeking help for a serious problem, asking for help is the first step towards positive progress.

* Individuals who would like to request a service, advice or donation – anyone in need of help.
* Charities looking to ask for specific help or donations – on an as and need basis, attracting people who perhaps cannot continually contribute their time or skills.
* Individuals, raising awareness of a social cause – not able to fit in to a local charity, giving their cause exposure.
* Start-up businesses who require help or guidance – high level of business fail, business should be helped too, especial since the government funding for support has been withdrawn.
* Individual who feels alone or lost, i.e. an elderly relative who need a one off request, a new mum who is new to the area.

The web application gives inquirers a free voice to be heard; asking is the first step towards receiving. Changes in modern society are considered to be the cause of many people feeling alone. We no longer live in nuclear family units, often living distances away from our extended family and friends.

The As and When Foundation web application will be the go to for any help required, bringing our society together, giving everyone the opportunity to ask for the specific help and therefore reaching more people who want to help and giving them an easy option to do so.

Charities and local communities events can have an opportunity to publish what they are doing locally and how people can get involved. The website will be free of charge to everyone! The volume of user will increase, starting locally and growing to expand across the UK.
